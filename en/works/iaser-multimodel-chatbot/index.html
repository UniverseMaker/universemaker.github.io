A Study of Multi-Model Chatbot Structure

다중 모델 챗봇 구조 연구

EEECS 2022 · 2022.07.20–22 · Lead author
Authors 박대승 (주저자) 외 공저
이 글의 상세 본문(구조·설계·기여)은 한국어로 제공됩니다 · Detailed sections below are in Korean

Proposes a chatbot architecture that routes among multiple models, each trained independently for a specialized domain.

Why it matters

A design that overcomes the expertise limits of a single model with multiple models.

Contribution
