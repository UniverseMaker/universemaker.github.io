A Study of BERT-Based Classification Performance of Text-Based Health Counseling Data

텍스트 기반 건강상담 데이터의 BERT 분류 성능 연구

CMES — Computer Modeling in Engineering & Sciences · 2022.09.29 · Co-author (2nd author) IF 2.9 JCR 2025
Authors Yeol Woo Sung, Dae Seung Park, Cheong Ghil Kim
이 글의 상세 본문(구조·설계·기여)은 한국어로 제공됩니다 · Detailed sections below are in Korean
AbstractThe entry into a hyper-connected society increases the generalization of communication using SNS. Therefore, research to analyze big data accumulated in SNS and extract meaningful information is being conducted in various fields. In particular, with the recent development of Deep Learning, the performance is rapidly improving by applying it to the field of Natural Language Processing, which is a language understanding technology to obtain accurate contextual information. In this paper, when a chatbot system is applied to the healthcare domain for counseling about diseases, the performance of NLP integrated with machine learning for the accurate classification of medical subjects from text-based health counseling data becomes important. Among the various algorithms, the performance of Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformers was compared with other algorithms of CNN, RNN, LSTM, and GRU. For this purpose, the health counseling data of Naver Q&A service were crawled as a dataset. KoBERT was used to classify medical subjects according to symptoms and the accuracy of classification results was measured. The simulation results show that KoBERT model performed high performance by more than 5% and close to 18% as large as the smallest.

Automatically classifies users' natural-language health-counseling posts into nine medical departments in a non-face-to-face care context, comparing BERT against CNN, LSTM, GRU, and RNN.

Structure · Design

전문의 답변으로 라벨링한 건강상담 텍스트를 9개 의과목으로 분류하는 다중 클래스 단일 라벨 문제. 동일 조건에서 RNN·GRU·LSTM·CNN·KoBERT를 비교하고, 정확도와 함께 재현율·정밀도·F1로 교차 검증한다.

Why it matters

It shows a pretrained language model (BERT) captures context even with little domain data and outperforms traditional RNN-family models — practical evidence for non-face-to-face medical routing.

Contribution

사전학습 언어모델(KoBERT)이 적은 도메인 데이터로도 문맥을 포착해 전통 RNN 계열을 능가함을 실증했다. 비대면 의료의 진료과 라우팅을 자동화하는 실용적 근거를 제공하되, 진단이 아닌 안내라는 경계를 분명히 한다.
