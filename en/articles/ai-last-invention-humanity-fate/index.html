Humanity's Last Invention: Artificial Intelligence and the Fate of Humankind

Nick Bostrom called artificial intelligence the last invention humanity will ever need to make. This column walks through the four stages — Narrow AI (ANI), General AI (AGI), Superintelligence (ASI) and Transintelligence (ATI) — and the questions that evolution puts to humankind.

DP
Daeseung Park AI & Software Systems Engineer · KAIST December 25, 2023 · 5 min read

If superintelligence exists, the fate of humanity will depend on it.

Artificial intelligence will be the last invention humanity ever needs to make.

Nick Bostrom, Professor of University of Oxford

Artificial intelligence (AI) has recently been growing explosively, and the pace of that growth keeps accelerating. In this piece I want to take a close look at the development of AI — which may well be humanity's last invention — and at what it means for humankind.

AI can be divided by kind and stage into four levels in total: Artificial Narrow Intelligence (ANI), Artificial General Intelligence (AGI), superintelligence or Artificial Superintelligence (ASI), and Artificial Transintelligence (ATI).

  1. Artificial Narrow Intelligence (ANI): AI at this stage focuses on a specific task or function. Systems such as IBM's Watson or Google's AlphaGo, for example, can be regarded as ANI. Such AI can surpass or assist human ability in a particular field, but its scope and understanding are very limited. It may perform brilliantly at a particular game such as chess or Go, yet it is helpless in any other domain.
  2. Artificial General Intelligence (AGI): AGI is the stage at which AI imitates general human intelligence. AI at this stage can solve many different kinds of problems and carry out human-like intellectual activities such as learning, understanding, reasoning and problem-solving. AGI can be applied flexibly across many fields and has the ability to learn and improve itself. AGI remains an unrealized goal for now, but research is advancing so quickly that scholars expect the age of AGI to arrive in the near future.
  3. Artificial Superintelligence (ASI): ASI is the stage at which AI far exceeds human intelligence. AI at this stage transcends humans not only in every intellectual activity humans can perform, but also in creative and complex problem-solving. ASI improves its own capabilities and can push out the frontier of intelligent activity. Its arrival could bring fundamental change to human civilization; for now, realizing AI at this stage lies in the very distant future.
  4. Artificial Transintelligence (ATI): ATI grows out of an ASI that has mastered human intelligence. It researches and develops, on its own, new knowledge that humans do not know, expanding the realm of human intellect — the final form of AI toward which humanity should leap in the very distant future.

Today's AI technology sits somewhere between ANI and AGI, and as OpenAI's ChatGPT and Google's Gemini & Bard advance at great speed, the age of AGI is predicted to arrive soon.

Yet many scholars and experts stress the dangers humanity will face as AI evolves beyond AGI into ASI and then ATI. The academic argument is that this evolution will surpass human intelligence, and that humanity may, as a result, come to be ruled by AI.

Experts expect humanity to reach the technological singularity around 2040–2050, and predict that it will be the moment at which humans merge with machines and develop into “superhumans.”

Academia warns that such optimism should be approached with caution. It emphasizes the potential risks and challenges that AI development could bring, arguing that they may become a grave problem for humanity.

Because AI is the only technology with a chance of becoming the first and last entity ever to surpass humanity, superintelligence could make humans god-like — or it could destroy us.

That is why we need, beyond mere technical forecasting, deep reflection on the future humanity must walk together with AI, and careful thought about both the benefits of AI development and the risks it may bring.
